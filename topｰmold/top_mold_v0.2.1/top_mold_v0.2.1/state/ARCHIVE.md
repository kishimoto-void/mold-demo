**Purpose:** retain eligible inactive history for continuity or audit. This file is not automatically included in every session and must not become a shadow copy of all conversations.

## Archive rules

- Only archive information that remains permitted to retain and has a clear future use or review purpose.
- Mark why an item became inactive: expired, superseded, task completed, or active-priority decay.
- Decay is not deletion. An archived item is inactive, not necessarily false.
- Do not archive sensitive information without explicit permission.
- A user deletion request is not an archive event. Remove accessible copies from active state, database, and archive as requested; do not preserve the deleted content in a change log.
- Do not infer a person's permanent identity from historical behavior.
